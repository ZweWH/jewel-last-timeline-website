THE SHARED MISSION

Waterfall service equipment must be lifted into place while the work area stays safe.

In an imagined work yard, the Construction Engineer plans and guides the lifts while the Safety Engineer checks the route and protects the lifting zone. Both must agree the area is ready before each lift.

These mission ideas and maps are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or finished game features.

Work together. Each player completes a role-specific part. Once both parts are done, each earns one jigsaw piece.
