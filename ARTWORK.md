# Concept artwork

The built-in image generation tool created four new fictional mission illustrations. These are concepts, not game captures or depictions of verified real-world services. Final assets are in `assets`.

Each prompt included: “Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: website illustration for a proposed student construction adventure mission. Cinematic, polished 3D game concept artwork, realistic materials, emerald green and warm gold accents, landscape composition, respectful safe PPE where appropriate. Fictional scene; no claim to show the real Jewel building or actual game. No text, lettering, logos, watermark, or UI.”

- `role-construction.png`: “A construction manager oversees a safe fictional lift of a large pump beside a glass-domed tropical garden construction site, crane hook, exclusion barriers, colleagues at a safe distance. Frame the pump and crane clearly.”
- `role-mep.png`: “An M&E coordinator inspects a fictional waterfall pump installation, tracing power and control connections between an electrical cabinet and pump equipment in a bright technical service area. Visually clear technical task.”
- `role-sustainability.png`: “A sustainability engineer compares a fictional waterfall water-reuse loop and energy choices on a tablet, beside a pump and simple storage tank, glass roof and tropical greenery visible in the distance. A practical engineering decision.”
- `role-architecture.png`: “An architectural coordinator checks a proposed service access panel and a concealed pipe route against a drawing in a finished tropical glass-roofed public space, keeping the walkway clear. Visualize maintenance access and design coordination.”

Other role illustrations and the time machine concept are reused from the existing website. The home banner uses the game main-menu illustration, adapted from the v095 menu artwork; it is not a gameplay capture.

The BIM illustration `assets/role-bim-engineer.png` was edited from the earlier BIM concept using the built-in image generation tool. Prompt: “Edit this exact existing construction mission concept illustration. Change only the lower-right inset heading text from ‘BIM COORDINATOR’ to exactly ‘BIM ENGINEER’, in the same dark green uppercase lettering, same position and style. Preserve everything else exactly, including image framing, jetpack character, pipes, duct, arrows, line art, colors, and all other text and buttons. Do not add or remove any other content.” The earlier illustration remains in the assets folder.

## Illustrated mission roadmaps

The ten `assets/roadmap-*.webp` images were generated with the built-in image tool using the approved BIM roadmap mockup as a style reference. The generated images were converted to WebP for faster loading. The shared prompt was: “Use the attached approved image only as a STYLE and mood reference, not as an image to copy literally. Create a new original portrait-format game level-map BACKGROUND for a website. [Role scene.] Polished charming isometric 3D construction-adventure artwork, miniature diorama, lush green vegetation, gold highlights, warm daylight, high detail, cohesive with the reference. Compose a clear central S-shaped open pathway winding from the bottom of the image through the middle to the top; leave the pathway open enough for a website to overlay five numbered interactive buttons later. No text, letters, numbers, logos, signs, badges, lock icons, UI controls, jigsaw icons, or existing game branding in the image. Do not draw markers or a dotted route; they will be added by the website. This is fictional concept art, not a real site plan or an existing gameplay screenshot.”

Role scenes: BIM — overhead pipes and ducts with a jetpack; Construction — crane, pump and safe lifting area; Technical — drawings, pump models and review table; Planning — road diversion, truck and barriers; QAQC — inspection route with joints and supports; Quantity — materials, fittings and a cost desk; M&E — pumps, pipes and electrical controls; Sustainability — reuse tanks, energy and greenery; Architect — public walkway, building models and access panels; Safety — protected route, exit and lifting zone. The numbered markers and route are website elements, not artwork or actual game progress.

## Exterior jigsaw picture

`assets/jigsaw-jewel-exterior-concept.webp` was created using the built-in image generation tool and converted losslessly to WebP. Prompt: “Create a clean landscape 2:1 illustration for a 50-piece physical jigsaw concept, viewed straight on from outside of the Jewel Changi Airport building in Singapore. Show the recognizable broad rounded glass-and-steel dome exterior and its surrounding tropical garden; emphasize the overall silhouette, with the complete building centered and safely away from all edges. This is a simplified illustrated exterior, not an interior view, no visible indoor waterfall or interior space. Design for possible future 3D modelling with four visually distinct solid flat colour regions only: dark forest green, light teal, warm cream and muted gold; broad connected colour shapes, thick simple structural lines, minimal detail, clear strong contrast, no gradients or shading or texture. Friendly premium poster / vector-style illustration, no perspective exaggeration. Sky and ground are simple large colour fields. Use only a handful of large stylized trees; no tiny leaves, no people, no text, logos, borders, jigsaw outlines, numbers or icons. Place all key visual content inside the central 70 percent of the canvas with generous top/bottom margins so the image can be cropped to 2:1 without cutting the building. This is only a picture for a concept, not a 3D render or an STL.”

The generated image is a visual reference rather than a fabrication file. The website adds the 50 piece outlines and labels separately.
