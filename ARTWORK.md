# Concept artwork

The built-in image generation tool created four new fictional mission illustrations. These are concepts, not game captures or depictions of verified real-world services. Final assets are in `assets`.

Each prompt included: “Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: website illustration for a proposed student construction adventure mission. Cinematic, polished 3D game concept artwork, realistic materials, emerald green and warm gold accents, landscape composition, respectful safe PPE where appropriate. Fictional scene; no claim to show the real Jewel building or actual game. No text, lettering, logos, watermark, or UI.”

- `role-construction.png`: “A construction manager oversees a safe fictional lift of a large pump beside a glass-domed tropical garden construction site, crane hook, exclusion barriers, colleagues at a safe distance. Frame the pump and crane clearly.”
- `role-mep.png`: “An M&E coordinator inspects a fictional waterfall pump installation, tracing power and control connections between an electrical cabinet and pump equipment in a bright technical service area. Visually clear technical task.”
- `role-sustainability.png`: “A sustainability engineer compares a fictional waterfall water-reuse loop and energy choices on a tablet, beside a pump and simple storage tank, glass roof and tropical greenery visible in the distance. A practical engineering decision.”
- `role-architecture.png`: “An architectural coordinator checks a proposed service access panel and a concealed pipe route against a drawing in a finished tropical glass-roofed public space, keeping the walkway clear. Visualize maintenance access and design coordination.”

Other role illustrations and the time machine concept are reused from the existing website. The home banner uses the game main-menu illustration, adapted from the v095 menu artwork; it is not a gameplay capture.

The BIM illustration `assets/role-bim-engineer.png` was edited from the earlier BIM concept using the built-in image generation tool. Prompt: “Edit this exact existing construction mission concept illustration. Change only the lower-right inset heading text from ‘BIM COORDINATOR’ to exactly ‘BIM ENGINEER’, in the same dark green uppercase lettering, same position and style. Preserve everything else exactly, including image framing, jetpack character, pipes, duct, arrows, line art, colors, and all other text and buttons. Do not add or remove any other content.” The earlier illustration remains in the assets folder.

## Illustrated mission roadmaps

The ten `assets/roadmap-*.webp` images were generated with the built-in image tool using the approved BIM roadmap mockup as a style reference. The generated images were converted to WebP for faster loading. The shared prompt was: “Use the attached approved image only as a STYLE and mood reference, not as an image to copy literally. Create a new original portrait-format game level-map BACKGROUND for a website. [Role scene.] Polished charming isometric 3D construction-adventure artwork, miniature diorama, lush green vegetation, gold highlights, warm daylight, high detail, cohesive with the reference. Compose a clear central S-shaped open pathway winding from the bottom of the image through the middle to the top; leave the pathway open enough for a website to overlay five numbered interactive buttons later. No text, letters, numbers, logos, signs, badges, lock icons, UI controls, jigsaw icons, or existing game branding in the image. Do not draw markers or a dotted route; they will be added by the website. This is fictional concept art, not a real site plan or an existing gameplay screenshot.”

Role scenes: BIM — overhead pipes and ducts with a jetpack; Construction — crane, pump and safe lifting area; Technical — drawings, pump models and review table; Planning — road diversion, truck and barriers; QAQC — inspection route with joints and supports; Quantity — materials, fittings and a cost desk; M&E — pumps, pipes and electrical controls; Sustainability — reuse tanks, energy and greenery; Architect — public walkway, building models and access panels; Safety — protected route, exit and lifting zone. The numbered markers and route are website elements, not artwork or actual game progress.

## Exterior jigsaw picture

`assets/jigsaw-jewel-exterior-concept.webp` was created using the built-in image generation tool and converted losslessly to WebP. Prompt: “Create a clean landscape 2:1 illustration for a 50-piece physical jigsaw concept, viewed straight on from outside of the Jewel Changi Airport building in Singapore. Show the recognizable broad rounded glass-and-steel dome exterior and its surrounding tropical garden; emphasize the overall silhouette, with the complete building centered and safely away from all edges. This is a simplified illustrated exterior, not an interior view, no visible indoor waterfall or interior space. Design for possible future 3D modelling with four visually distinct solid flat colour regions only: dark forest green, light teal, warm cream and muted gold; broad connected colour shapes, thick simple structural lines, minimal detail, clear strong contrast, no gradients or shading or texture. Friendly premium poster / vector-style illustration, no perspective exaggeration. Sky and ground are simple large colour fields. Use only a handful of large stylized trees; no tiny leaves, no people, no text, logos, borders, jigsaw outlines, numbers or icons. Place all key visual content inside the central 70 percent of the canvas with generous top/bottom margins so the image can be cropped to 2:1 without cutting the building. This is only a picture for a concept, not a 3D render or an STL.”

`assets/jigsaw-jewel-exterior-detailed.webp` is an image-tool edit of that illustration, converted losslessly to WebP. Prompt: “Edit this existing illustration for a 50-piece jigsaw concept. Keep the same wide 2:1 composition, the same limited dark forest green, mint teal, warm cream and golden yellow palette and flat vector-like look, and clearly recognizable EXTERIOR view of Jewel Changi Airport. Make it a little more complex and complete than the reference: add extra architectural glass panels, a more developed entrance with layered walkways and landscaping, some layered trees and shrubs, a small suggestion of surrounding ground or paths, and a little extra building depth. Balanced attractive construction-themed illustration, polished and clear at small size. Keep the forms broad, bold, separated and simple enough to serve as inspiration for a future multi-colour 3D-printed physical puzzle; avoid fine texture, gradients, photographs, tiny details, people, 3D perspective interior, puzzle lines or numbers. Leave a generous clean warm cream open sky area at upper left so an authentic logo can be added separately. No text and no logos; do not invent a logo.” The authentic Woh Hup logo and wordmark are added as a separate image layer using `assets/wohhup-official-logo-transparent.png`, copied from the project’s existing reference artwork. The result is a picture concept, not a printable 3D model.

The generated image is a visual reference rather than a fabrication file. The website adds the 50 piece outlines and labels separately.

## Top-down role roadmap concepts

The ten `assets/roadmap-topdown-*.webp` images were made with the built-in image generation tool, one image per role, then converted to WebP. They are fictional background illustrations; the route markers, level descriptions, and interface are implemented by the website, not baked into the images.

For BIM, Construction, Technical, Planning, and QAQC, the common prompt was: “Create a portrait 2D construction-adventure level-map BACKGROUND for a website. Absolute strict orthographic plan view, camera pointing straight down exactly 90 degrees like a 2D map editor: NO perspective, isometric, depth rendering, building facades, humans, or sky. Thematic visual style: refined playful hand-drawn flat-vector game map, deep forest green outlines, warm ivory and pale green surfaces, teal water, muted gold highlights; sophisticated and easy to scan, same cohesive illustration palette throughout. A broad, continuous light-cream S-shaped walking route winds from bottom center toward top center, with five distinct zones placed along it. Route must be clear and open so HTML can overlay controls. Fictional training map, not a real Jewel floorplan. No text, letters, numerals, icons of jigsaw pieces, UI, pins, markers, badges or logos. Make all objects clearly overhead plan symbols, stylized and detailed but with readable flat forms.” The following scene descriptions were appended:

- BIM: “Scene: a fictional basement services coordination map with overhead blue water pipes, grey air ducts, a structural beam, cable tray, pipe bends and a valve service area; five clearly differentiated fictional inspection zones around the winding path.”
- Construction: “Scene: a fictional construction lifting yard map with the top-down footprints of a mobile crane and lifting zone, pump equipment, staged pipe sections, safety barriers and destination pads; five clearly differentiated work areas around the winding path.”
- Technical: “Scene: a fictional technical engineering review map with drawing desks, pump tags, pipe plans, specification sheets, revision drawings and equipment diagrams shown as neat overhead plan symbols; five clearly differentiated review stations around the winding path.”
- Planning: “Scene: a fictional overhead construction site traffic diversion map with roads, construction zones, delivery trucks, orange-and-white barriers, pedestrian crossing and emergency exit lanes; five clearly differentiated road phases around the winding path.”
- QAQC: “Scene: a fictional quality inspection route with pipe junctions, seals, a pump, equipment tags, pipe supports and inspection tables, all in plan view; five clearly differentiated inspection stations around the winding path.”

For Quantity Surveyor, M&E, Sustainability, Architect, and Safety, the common prompt was: “Create ONE portrait 2:3 background for a construction adventure videogame website roadmap. Strict true 2D TOP-DOWN orthographic view, camera points straight down at 90 degrees like a hand-illustrated level select game map or architectural plan. No perspective, no isometric, no visible vertical building faces. Charming polished flat illustrated game-map look, clear stylized bold forms, highly legible at web size, cohesive colors deep forest green, mint teal, warm cream and muted gold, with subtle construction-site accents. Fictional concept, not a real plan. Five distinct activity areas along a broad pale winding S-shaped route that runs bottom-to-top, with open areas for the website to place interactive level text alongside. [Role-specific scenes.] No characters, no lettered words, no numbers, no labels, no badges, no interface panels, no arrows, no watermark; all level markers and labels will be added by the website later.” The following role-specific scenes were inserted:

- Quantity Surveyor: “A waterfall-services construction quantity survey map with top-down plan tables, measured pipe lengths, fittings, pump equipment, cost documents and delivery pallets; all objects seen directly from above.”
- M&E: “A fictional water-systems service level map with top-down pumps, valves, pipes, cable routes, electrical cabinets and a small water tank, all viewed as a plan.”
- Sustainability: “A water and energy sustainability level map with top-down rainwater collection tanks, recycling stations, water pipes, solar panels and green landscaped pockets.”
- Architect: “An architectural coordination site map with overhead walls, rooms, access panels, equipment clearances, ceiling plan areas and pedestrian paths.”
- Safety: “A construction safety inspection site map with overhead barriers, marked lifting zone, open edge protection, cable trip hazards, an emergency exit route and safety signs as symbols only.”
