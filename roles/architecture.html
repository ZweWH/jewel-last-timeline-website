MISSION 09 / CONCEPT

Architect

Make space for the people and the services.

10 ROLES · ONE TEAMPROPOSED MISSIONFICTIONAL STORY

THE MISSION

A service access panel conflicts with a finished public space.

Find a way for the fictional waterfall services to be maintained while respecting the design and keeping the public route clear.

This mission is an idea for the next version. The scene and artwork are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or a finished game feature.

THREE ISSUES TO SOLVE

Start with one star and work your way up to three. Each issue is a proposed game idea.

  1. ★☆☆ 1 STAR · EASIER

    Hidden access panel

    A finish covers a panel needed to reach a service valve.

    What you do: Choose a panel position that allows the valve to be reached.

  2. ★★☆ 2 STARS · INTERMEDIATE

    Pipe meets the finish

    A proposed pipe route crosses a finished wall feature.

    What you do: Compare the options and select a route that fits the design and the services.

  3. ★★★ 3 STARS · HARDER

    Space for everyone

    A maintenance panel opens into a busy public route.

    What you do: Choose a revised panel and route that keep both maintenance access and the walkway clear.

YOUR KEY

After solving all three issues: A checked access solution earns the Architect key for the Director.

Work as a team. Coordinate the proposed access with BIM and Safety.
