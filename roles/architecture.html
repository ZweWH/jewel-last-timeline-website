Architect

Make space for the people and the services.

FIVE LEVELS · FIVE PIECESPROPOSED MISSIONFICTIONAL STORY

THE MISSION

A service access panel conflicts with a finished public space.

Find a way for the fictional waterfall services to be maintained while respecting the design and keeping the public route clear.

These mission ideas and maps are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or finished game features.

Work as a team. Coordinate the proposed access with BIM and Safety.
