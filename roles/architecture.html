THE SHARED MISSION

The waterfall service needs a balanced plan that fits its surroundings and stays accessible.

In fictional Jewel spaces, the Sustainability Engineer compares water, energy and material choices while the Architect checks access and the public route. Together they choose options that meet the supplied targets and fit the design.

These mission ideas and maps are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or finished game features.

Work together. Each player completes a role-specific part. Once both parts are done, each earns one jigsaw piece.
