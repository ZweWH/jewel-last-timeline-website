THE MISSION

Hazards are blocking the team from reaching the waterfall work area.

Inspect a fictional construction route and make it safe for the team. Check every control before reopening access.

This mission is an idea for the next version. The scene and artwork are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or a finished game feature.

HOW THE MISSION COULD WORK

  1. 01

    Find hazards at the edge, exit and lifting zone.

  2. 02

    Choose and place the right safety controls.

  3. 03

    Recheck the controls and walk the protected route.

YOUR KEY

A verified safe route earns the Safety key for the Director.

Work as a team. Coordinate the lifting zone with the Construction Manager and safe access with Archi.
