Safety Engineer

Make the route safe.

10 ROLES · ONE TEAMPROPOSED MISSIONFICTIONAL STORY

THE MISSION

Hazards are blocking the team from reaching the waterfall work area.

Inspect a fictional construction route and make it safe for the team. Check every control before reopening access.

This mission is an idea for the next version. The scene and artwork are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or a finished game feature.

THREE ISSUES TO SOLVE

Start with one star and work your way up to three. Each issue is a proposed game idea.

  1. ★☆☆ 1 STAR · EASIER

    Blocked exit

    Materials block the exit on the way to the waterfall work area.

    What you do: Identify the obstruction, choose a control and recheck the exit.

  2. ★★☆ 2 STARS · INTERMEDIATE

    Unprotected edge

    A work route passes an edge without protection.

    What you do: Select and place the supplied edge protection, then inspect it.

  3. ★★★ 3 STARS · HARDER

    Lift crosses the route

    A planned lifting zone overlaps the team’s access route.

    What you do: Set an exclusion zone and a safe diversion, then check that the team and load remain separated.

YOUR KEY

After solving all three issues: A verified safe route earns the Safety key for the Director.

Work as a team. Coordinate the lifting zone with the Construction Engineer and safe access with the Architect.
