THE MISSION

The pump and its power and controls need to work together.

Trace the fictional mechanical and electrical connections for a waterfall service. Find the missing connection and check the system before a test.

This mission is an idea for the next version. The scene and artwork are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or a finished game feature.

HOW THE MISSION COULD WORK

  1. 01

    Trace the pump, power and control connections.

  2. 02

    Match and connect the supplied components.

  3. 03

    Run a safe test and check the response.

YOUR KEY

A checked system test earns the M&E key for the Director.

Work as a team. Use BIM route information and QAQC inspection results to guide the work.
