THE MISSION

The pump and its power and controls need to work together.

Trace the fictional mechanical and electrical connections for a waterfall service. Find the missing connection and check the system before a test.

These mission ideas and maps are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or finished game features.

Work as a team. Use BIM route information and QAQC inspection results to guide the work.
