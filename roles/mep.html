THE SHARED MISSION

A fictional waterfall service needs correct connections and a careful inspection before testing.

In an imagined service bay, the M&E Engineer checks the water, power and control connections while the QAQC Engineer looks for installation defects. They compare the supplied records and recheck each fix together.

These mission ideas and maps are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or finished game features.

Work together. Each player completes a role-specific part. Once both parts are done, each earns one jigsaw piece.
