M&E Engineer

Connect the systems.

THE MISSION

The pump and its power and controls need to work together.

Trace the fictional mechanical and electrical connections for a waterfall service. Find the missing connection and check the system before a test.

This mission is an idea for the next version. The scene and artwork are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or a finished game feature.

THREE ISSUES TO SOLVE

Start with one star and work your way up to three. Each issue is a proposed game idea.

  1. ★☆☆ 1 STAR · EASIER

    Missing valve

    A valve is missing from the fictional water circuit.

    What you do: Trace the marked pipe and choose the matching valve for the gap.

  2. ★★☆ 2 STARS · INTERMEDIATE

    Power connection

    The pump is not linked to its supplied power connection.

    What you do: Follow the diagram and match the pump to the correct power connection.

  3. ★★★ 3 STARS · HARDER

    Control mismatch

    The pump has power but does not respond to the test control.

    What you do: Compare the fictional control links with the diagram, correct the mismatch and run the test again.

YOUR KEY

After solving all three issues: A checked system test earns the M&E key for the Director.

Work as a team. Use BIM route information and QAQC inspection results to guide the work.
