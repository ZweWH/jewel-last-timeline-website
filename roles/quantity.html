Quantity Surveyor

Count what the job needs.

10 ROLES · ONE TEAMPROPOSED MISSIONFICTIONAL STORY

THE MISSION

The team needs a complete waterfall service package within a training budget.

Use a fictional drawing and supplied prices to work out what the project needs. A low price is not useful if important parts are missing.

This mission is an idea for the next version. The scene and artwork are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or a finished game feature.

HOW THE MISSION COULD WORK

  1. 01

    Measure the pipes, fittings and equipment on the drawing.

  2. 02

    Calculate the cost using the supplied rates.

  3. 03

    Compare quotes and select a complete package.

YOUR KEY

A checked package and cost earn the Quantity Surveyor key for the Director.

Work as a team. Confirm the required items with Technical and M&E.
