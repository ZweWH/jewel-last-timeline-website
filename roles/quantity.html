MISSION 06 / CONCEPT

Quantity Surveyor

Count what the job needs.

10 ROLES · ONE TEAMPROPOSED MISSIONFICTIONAL STORY

THE MISSION

The team needs a complete waterfall service package within a training budget.

Use a fictional drawing and supplied prices to work out what the project needs. A low price is not useful if important parts are missing.

This mission is an idea for the next version. The scene and artwork are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or a finished game feature.

THREE ISSUES TO SOLVE

Start with one star and work your way up to three. Each issue is a proposed game idea.

  1. ★☆☆ 1 STAR · EASIER

    Count the fittings

    The service package list is missing fittings.

    What you do: Count the fittings shown on the supplied drawing and complete the list.

  2. ★★☆ 2 STARS · INTERMEDIATE

    Measure and price

    A pipe run has no quantity or cost.

    What you do: Measure its length on the drawing and calculate its cost using the supplied rate.

  3. ★★★ 3 STARS · HARDER

    Choose a complete quote

    One cheap quote leaves out parts needed for the waterfall service.

    What you do: Compare quantities and prices, then select a complete package within the training budget.

YOUR KEY

After solving all three issues: A checked package and cost earn the Quantity Surveyor key for the Director.

Work as a team. Confirm the required items with Technical and M&E.
