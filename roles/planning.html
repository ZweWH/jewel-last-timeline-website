Planning Engineer

Make a route. Keep the work moving.

FIVE LEVELS · FIVE PIECESPROPOSED MISSIONFICTIONAL STORY

THE MISSION

A changing construction site is blocking a waterfall equipment delivery.

Work on a top-down 2D site map. Plan a road diversion for the current construction phase while keeping important access clear.

These mission ideas and maps are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or finished game features.

Work as a team. Tell the Construction Engineer when equipment can reach the lifting area.
