Planning Engineer

Make a route. Keep the work moving.

10 ROLES · ONE TEAMPROPOSED MISSIONFICTIONAL STORY

THE MISSION

A changing construction site is blocking a waterfall equipment delivery.

Work on a top-down 2D site map. Plan a road diversion for the current construction phase while keeping important access clear.

This mission is an idea for the next version. The scene and artwork are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or a finished game feature.

THREE ISSUES TO SOLVE

Start with one star and work your way up to three. Each issue is a proposed game idea.

  1. ★☆☆ 1 STAR · EASIER

    One closed road

    A work area blocks the direct route for a waterfall part.

    What you do: On the top-down 2D map, place a barrier and arrows, then test the delivery vehicle.

  2. ★★☆ 2 STARS · INTERMEDIATE

    Keep access open

    The next construction phase closes another road near an emergency route.

    What you do: Move the barriers and arrows to create a delivery route that keeps emergency access clear, then test it.

  3. ★★★ 3 STARS · HARDER

    A changing site

    A new work zone blocks the planned route just before the pump delivery.

    What you do: Revise the diversion, protect the emergency route and test the vehicle through the updated layout.

YOUR KEY

After solving all three issues: A tested delivery route earns the Planning key for the Director.

Work as a team. Tell the Construction Manager when equipment can reach the lifting area.
