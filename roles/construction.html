Construction Manager

Plan the lift. Keep everyone safe.

10 ROLES · ONE TEAMPROPOSED MISSIONFICTIONAL STORY

THE MISSION

Waterfall equipment needs to reach its marked position safely and on time.

Lead a series of fictional lifting activities. Check the plan, protect the work area and guide the equipment into place before time runs out.

This mission is an idea for the next version. The scene and artwork are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or a finished game feature.

THREE ISSUES TO SOLVE

Start with one star and work your way up to three. Each issue is a proposed game idea.

  1. ★☆☆ 1 STAR · EASIER

    Move a service part

    A service component is waiting at the delivery area.

    What you do: Check the lifting plan, clear the zone and guide it to its marked position.

  2. ★★☆ 2 STARS · INTERMEDIATE

    Lift the pump

    A larger pump must pass a blocked part of the work area.

    What you do: Choose a clear load path, set the barriers and guide the pump safely into place.

  3. ★★★ 3 STARS · HARDER

    Lifts against the clock

    Several waterfall service parts must reach different positions before time runs out.

    What you do: Plan the order and complete the lifts in time, stopping whenever the lifting zone becomes unsafe.

YOUR KEY

After solving all three issues: Safe, checked lifts earn the Construction Manager key for the Director.

Work as a team. Coordinate safe access with Safety and delivery timing with Planning.
