THE MISSION

The waterfall equipment information does not match across the drawing and records.

Review the fictional design information before the team installs the equipment. Find the differences and choose the correct details.

These mission ideas and maps are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or finished game features.

Work as a team. Share the checked details with BIM and M&E.
