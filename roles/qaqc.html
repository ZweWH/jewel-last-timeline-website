THE MISSION

The fictional waterfall services have defects that must be fixed before handover.

Inspect the installation carefully. A leaking connection, an incomplete seal or a wrong tag could delay the return of the flow.

This mission is an idea for the next version. The scene and artwork are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or a finished game feature.

THREE ISSUES TO SOLVE

Start with one star and work your way up to three. Each issue is a proposed game idea.

  1. ★☆☆ 1 STAR · EASIER

    Missing seal

    A service opening has an incomplete seal.

    What you do: Find it, select the supplied repair and reinspect the seal.

  2. ★★☆ 2 STARS · INTERMEDIATE

    Leaking connection

    A pipe joint leaks during a fictional inspection.

    What you do: Locate the leak, choose the repair and check the joint again.

  3. ★★★ 3 STARS · HARDER

    Wrong component

    An installed fitting does not match the checked drawing.

    What you do: Compare the fitting and records, select the correct replacement and reinspect the work.

YOUR KEY

After solving all three issues: A passed reinspection earns the QAQC key for the Director.

Work as a team. Share the inspection results with M&E and Technical.
