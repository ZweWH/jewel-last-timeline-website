THE MISSION

The fictional waterfall services have defects that must be fixed before handover.

Inspect the installation carefully. A leaking connection, an incomplete seal or a wrong tag could delay the return of the flow.

This mission is an idea for the next version. The scene and artwork are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or a finished game feature.

HOW THE MISSION COULD WORK

  1. 01

    Inspect the service installation for defects.

  2. 02

    Choose a repair for each problem.

  3. 03

    Reinspect the repairs and approve the handover.

YOUR KEY

A passed reinspection earns the QAQC key for the Director.

Work as a team. Share the inspection results with M&E and Technical.
