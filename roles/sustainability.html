Sustainability Engineer

Restore the flow wisely.

10 ROLES · ONE TEAMPROPOSED MISSIONFICTIONAL STORY

THE MISSION

The waterfall needs a practical plan for using water and energy.

Compare fictional operating choices and their effects. Choose a balanced plan that supports the waterfall while reducing waste.

This mission is an idea for the next version. The scene and artwork are concepts, not proof of the real Jewel’s services or a finished game feature.

THREE ISSUES TO SOLVE

Start with one star and work your way up to three. Each issue is a proposed game idea.

  1. ★☆☆ 1 STAR · EASIER

    Water being wasted

    A fictional water-use report shows an unnecessary loss.

    What you do: Compare the supplied choices and select a way to reduce the waste.

  2. ★★☆ 2 STARS · INTERMEDIATE

    Energy choice

    Two pump settings use different amounts of energy.

    What you do: Compare the supplied energy and flow figures and choose a setting that meets the target.

  3. ★★★ 3 STARS · HARDER

    Balance the plan

    The lowest-energy choice does not meet the required water flow.

    What you do: Compare the supplied water and energy trade-offs and select a balanced operating plan.

YOUR KEY

After solving all three issues: A checked operating plan earns the Sustainability key for the Director.

Work as a team. Share the chosen plan with M&E for the system test.
